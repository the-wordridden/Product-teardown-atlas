**Figma can show how it grows inside an account. It can't show how it wins the account.** A research pass aimed squarely at the loop evidenced one link in five, and that link sits inside customers who already pay.

## Around the chain

**Getting to acquisition.** The 10-K names two ways in, self serve and direct sales <Evidence id="gtm.two-motions" />, and a public Community page anyone can reach, with a Remix button <Evidence id="community.public-surface" />. The filing also claims "product virality inherent to Figma's collaborative and browser-based platform." I went looking for that mechanism specifically and found nothing behind it.

**Acquisition to activation.** Nothing defines activation, and opening a privately shared file without an account is something I couldn't observe.

**Activation to core action.** The free View seat lets anyone look and comment <Evidence id="seat.view.free" />. Whether viewers move toward editing isn't shown.

**Core action to output.** That design work reaches non-users is inferred from the product's shape, not observed.

**Output to reinvestment: proven.** Free viewers inside a paying account become billed seats through a path that can be set to approve itself <Evidence id="seat.approval-settings" />. Practitioners confirm it from 2021 to 2025 <Evidence id="seat.upgrade-billing.practitioner" />, and the numbers show it: 136% net dollar retention <Evidence id="ndr.q2-2026" /> and 34% growth in the over-$10,000 group <Evidence id="paid-customers.10k.q2-2026" />.

**Reinvestment back to acquisition.** Revenue funds R&D and AI <Evidence id="rnd.fy2025" />. Spending on R&D isn't proof that R&D acquires anyone.

## What that means

Every practitioner story involves an admin, an invoice and a subscription that already existed. Growing seats isn't winning customers. What the evidence supports is an **expansion engine**: strong, documented, low friction, and fenced in by accounts that already pay.

<Judgment>
The gap between Figma's stated growth story and its evidenced mechanism is the most interesting thing this research found. A filing claims virality; a focused search found expansion and no acquisition. That doesn't make the claim false. It means it rests on Figma's word alone, and I'd weight it that way.
</Judgment>
