**No moat mechanism has been established from the available evidence.** That's not "Figma has no moat", which would overclaim in the other direction. A moat here needs a named mechanism, evidence it operates, a reason it's hard to copy, and a way to attack it. Four candidates clear the first bar. None clears the rest.

- **Collaboration.** The multiplayer architecture is documented in unusual detail by Figma itself <Evidence id="architecture.multiplayer.2019" />. The capability is real, and so is the public roadmap for rebuilding it.
- **Technical architecture.** Serious engineering, publicly described, and seven years old as evidence.
- **Ecosystem.** A large public plugin and template catalogue <Evidence id="community.public-surface" />, built largely on open protocols. The openness that grows it lowers the cost of a rival one.
- **Market position.** The UK regulator found Figma "the clear market leader… several times larger than any other supplier" in 2023 <Evidence id="cma.market-position.2023" />. Position says where a company stands. A moat says why it can't be moved.

## What I won't blur

**Collaboration isn't a network effect.** Nothing shows the first person's value rising because a specific second person joined.

**A feature isn't a switching cost.** Design system management exists <Evidence id="product-surface.10k" />, but a switching cost needs the friction felt and the cost paid. I found no account of an organisation trying to leave.

**Retention isn't defensibility.** 136% <Evidence id="ndr.q2-2026" /> can't tell you which of those forces is at work.

## The counter-evidence

Figma's own risk factors name "design-to-code and AI-driven companies", point tools that "can expand to cover more", and customers' internal tools, and admit pricing power "may be constrained by competitive dynamics" <Evidence id="ai-margin-warning.fy2025" />. Several of those sit outside the CMA's 2023 market definition, so the independent evidence of leadership was drawn against a boundary Figma's own filings no longer accept.
