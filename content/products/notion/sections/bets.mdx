Three bets. One paid off, one is unfalsifiable by design, and one keeps costing something Notion doesn't account for.

## Give it away to one person: paid off

In May 2020 Notion removed the block cap for individuals <Evidence id="block.limit.history.2020" />; it now appears only at the second member <Evidence id="block.limit.free" />. The free individuals made the templates. The gallery went from 600 to 30,000, creators from about 2,000 to 22,000 <Evidence id="gallery.scale.2023" /> <Evidence id="gallery.scale.2026" />, with 51 million duplications in a year <Evidence id="gallery.duplications.2023" />.

The cost: a revenue line, and serving free users at block granularity, which took 480 Postgres shards <Evidence id="block.sharding.2021" />. It's the first time in this Atlas I've marked a bet **paid off**; the results are Notion's own and unambiguous.

## Make AI the tier: unresolved

Full AI sits behind the $20 plan, with credits for premium models <Evidence id="ai.plan-gate.2026" />, and Notion 3.0 agents do "up to 20 minutes of autonomous work at a time" <Evidence id="agents.2025" />. The upgrade question stops being "do I want AI" and becomes "do I want the better plan."

<Judgment>
The packaging that makes this good business makes it impossible to evaluate. If AI is bundled into a tier, every Business seat is an "AI-enabled customer" <Evidence id="ai.arr-share.2026" />, and the category expands to fit. I'd want AI-attributable revenue separated from tier revenue before calling this bet working.
</Judgment>

## Buy the team, switch off the product: costing trust

Skiff raised $14.2 million for encrypted docs and mail. Notion bought it in 2024 and shut it down within a year, converting no accounts <Evidence id="acquisition.skiff.2024" />. Before that came Flowdash, Automate.io and Cron (now Notion Calendar); in July 2026, ZeroEntropy, whose founder now leads a Model Research group <Evidence id="acquisition.zeroentropy.2026" />. Five acquisitions, no disclosed prices.

Buying people and skipping the second codebase is sound. But a company whose main moat is that leaving is hard <Evidence id="lockin.anxiety.2020" /> keeps demonstrating that products it owns can be turned off.
