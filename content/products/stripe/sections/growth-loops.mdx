Stripe's loop closes on paper. Each payment leaves something behind, and each thing left behind is priced. The catch is that every rate on it comes from Stripe, and the one stretch with independent evidence is where the stories are about accounts being held.

## Around the loop

**Signup to first charge: documented.** No contract, no fixed fee, so the path to a first live payment is the integration itself <Evidence id="pricing.us.2026" />. Stripe seeds it from AI coding tools, with over 100,000 sandboxes that convert to live accounts <Evidence id="sandboxes.2025" />, and says 20% of Atlas companies charged a customer within 30 days <Evidence id="atlas.2025" />.

**First charge to ongoing processing: not shown.** Nothing measures how many activated businesses keep processing or reach real volume. The only independent evidence is practitioners describing holds and closures <Evidence id="hn.account-holds" />. That's the gap.

**Processing to Link: documented.** Each Link checkout enrols the consumer with Stripe, so they're recognised at the next merchant <Evidence id="docs.link.enrolment" />. Lovable takes 58% of its volume through Link <Evidence id="lovable.link-share.2026" />. Stripe's claim that Radar gets better with every payment <Evidence id="radar.network.claim.2024" /> isn't counted: nobody outside Stripe has measured it.

**Data to attached products: documented.** Capital is underwritten on the account's own history <Evidence id="docs.capital.underwriting" />, and Billing, Tax and Radar are priced on volume already flowing <Evidence id="pricing.products.2026" />. Attach rates aren't disclosed.

**Back to acquisition: documented, weakly.** A Standard connected account on a platform is a full Stripe account <Evidence id="docs.connect.standard-accounts" />, and businesses on Stripe pay each other 4.8 million times a day <Evidence id="b2b-payments.daily.2026" />. No source says how many accounts arrived that way.

<Judgment>
The most interesting thing in this loop is a node, not a link: Link. Two hundred million consumers enrolled with Stripe rather than with any merchant is an asset that grows with every checkout and, as Moats shows, doesn't leave when a merchant does. If the loop works the way the letters imply, that's the part turning it.
</Judgment>
