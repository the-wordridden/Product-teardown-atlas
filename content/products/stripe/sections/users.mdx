Stripe describes its users by size: all the top AI companies, 90% of the Dow and 80% of the Nasdaq 100 <Evidence id="enterprise-penetration.2025" />, and a quarter of new Delaware corporations through Atlas <Evidence id="atlas.2025" />. Those percentages count any use of any product, so they say Stripe is everywhere, not who pays it most. The segments below are grouped by how customers arrive and how they're billed.

**Developer-led software businesses.** The 2011 audience, still the one Stripe writes for. They sign up themselves and pay per charge. The 2025 cohort was the fastest-growing Stripe has seen, and 57% of it was outside the US <Evidence id="cohort.international.2025" />. Two newer front doors feed this group: over 100,000 sandboxes created from AI coding tools <Evidence id="sandboxes.2025" /> and Atlas incorporations.

**Platforms and marketplaces.** Shopify's stores, Airbnb's hosts, DoorDash's restaurants. Connect is priced separately, at $2 per active account and 0.25% + 25¢ per payout <Evidence id="pricing.connect.2026" />, and the docs spell out who carries the risk: the platform, on destination charges and on legacy Express and Custom accounts <Evidence id="docs.connect.charge-types" />.

**Enterprises on negotiated terms.** They don't pay 2.9% <Evidence id="pricing.products.2026" />. In the UK subsidiary, sales is the largest team, 128 of 288 <Evidence id="spuk.employees.fy2024" />.
