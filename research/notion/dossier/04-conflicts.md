# Notion — Conflicts and Definition Drift

Per §5 and §9 of the methodology.

---

## C1 — "ARR from AI-enabled customers" vs "ARR from AI products"

**Sources.** S005 (Notion's own post, 2026-01-26): "more than 50% of our ARR came from AI-enabled customers, and that percentage more than doubled over the last year." S006 (Forbes, 2025-12-15): "recently passed $600 million in annual recurring revenue, half of which is coming from its artificial intelligence products."

**Step 1 — same metric?** No. Notion's sentence measures **customers who use AI**, and counts all of their subscription revenue. Forbes's sentence measures **revenue earned from AI products**. A workspace paying $20/seat for Business and using AI Meeting Notes contributes 100% of its revenue to Notion's measure and an unknown, possibly zero, amount to Forbes's.

**Step 2 — same period?** Roughly: Forbes is 2025-12-15, Notion is 2026-01-26, both describing the same tender.

**Step 3 — arithmetic.** Cannot distinguish; both round to "half".

**Step 4 — corroboration.** None opened.

**Resolution.** **Preserved as a conflict, and the distinction is the finding.** Publish Notion's wording as Notion's and Forbes's as Forbes's, never merged into "half of Notion's revenue is AI". The gap between the two sentences is exactly the kind of blur this Atlas exists to refuse. Open question 6.

## C2 — Tender size: ~$270M vs ~$300M

**Sources.** S005: "total tender of around $270M at an $11B valuation" (2026-01-26). S006: "the deal is expected to be for about $300 million worth of shares" (2025-12-15).

**Assessment.** Not a contradiction. Forbes reported the intended size at kickoff; Notion reported the completed size six weeks later. Tender offers are sized by how many employees participate.

**Resolution.** Cite $270M as the completed figure from the company, note the ~$300M expectation at announcement. Valuation ($11B) agrees across both.

## C3 — Aggregator figures with no primary source

**Sources.** Search results surfaced, without any primary attribution: "100M users", "4M customers", "~1,000 employees", "$500M ARR", "$343.2M total funding", "$418M total funding". Two of those funding totals contradict each other, and the ARR figure contradicts both opened sources.

**Resolution.** **None admitted to the register.** Methodology §12.3 rule 1: a figure that reaches me through an aggregator is a lead, not evidence. Recorded here so the absence is deliberate and traceable, and so a later pass does not "rediscover" them as new.
