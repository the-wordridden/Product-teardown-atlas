## Assessment

**Four mechanisms are EVIDENCED as capabilities. None is evidenced as a moat.**

The distinction carries the whole section. A capability is something the product has. A moat is a reason a competitor with capital and talent cannot take the customer. Every mechanism above clears the first bar; none clears the second, because in each case the replication barrier is either publicly documented (collaboration architecture, sync design), openly standardised (MCP, integrations), or simply unmeasured (switching costs, accumulated assets).

**The strongest defensibility-adjacent evidence remains the CMA's 2023 market position finding** — and it is a position, established under a market definition that Figma's own 2026 risk factors no longer appear to accept.

**Three mechanisms cannot be assessed at all** because the §6.4 test fails at step 2 (`09`).

Per the brief, **no ranking is offered.**
