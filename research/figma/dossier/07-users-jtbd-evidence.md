## 4. Why this gap did not close

JTBD requires evidence about **users**, and every strong source in this dossier is about the **company**. Filings, pricing pages, engineering posts and regulatory documents describe what Figma built and what it earns; none describes what a user was struggling with beforehand.

The forum evidence located in Stage 1C [S013, S015] is genuinely about users — but about *billing frustration*, not about hiring the product. It bears on the seat mechanism (`11`), not on jobs.

**Closing this requires a different source class than anything gathered so far:** user interviews, structured review corpora, adoption case studies, or direct product observation with an authenticated account. The first three were searched for in this pass and not found in usable form; the fourth is blocked (`08`).

**Recommendation: do not author Section 4.** A JTBD section written from packaging inference would be exactly the "polished but somewhat bullshit" output this methodology exists to prevent.
