Stripe is a private company run from San Francisco and Dublin <Evidence id="hq.dublin-sf" />, founded in 2010 and launched to the public in September 2011 <Evidence id="pricing.launch.2011" />. It has never filed audited group accounts, so every headline number on this page comes from Stripe's own letters or from journalists relaying people who have seen the books. I've labelled them that way and I'd ask you to read them that way.

Here's what Stripe says about itself. Businesses on Stripe moved $1.9 trillion in 2025, up 34% <Evidence id="tpv.2025" />, which Stripe puts at roughly 1.6% of global GDP <Evidence id="gdp-share.2025" />. More than 5 million businesses use it directly or through platforms <Evidence id="businesses.2025" />. It was profitable in 2024 and expected to stay so <Evidence id="profitable.2024" />, and "robustly" so in 2025 <Evidence id="profitable.2025" />.

Here's what other people say. The Information reported 2024 revenue of $5.1 billion, up 28%, with free cash flow doubling to $2.2 billion <Evidence id="revenue.2024.reported" /> <Evidence id="fcf.2024.reported" />, and put 2025 free cash flow at $3.2 billion in a headline I could read but a body I couldn't <Evidence id="fcf.2025.reported" />. Investors priced the company at $159 billion in February 2026 <Evidence id="valuation.feb-2026" />, up from $91.5 billion a year earlier <Evidence id="valuation.feb-2025" /> and a $50 billion low in 2023 <Evidence id="series-i.2023" />.

## The one audited window

There is one place where a real auditor has signed. Stripe Payments UK Ltd, the entity the FCA authorises as an e-money institution, files full accounts at Companies House. For 2024, EY signed off turnover of £896.5 million, up 28% <Evidence id="spuk.turnover.fy2024" />, with 288 employees of whom 128 were in sales <Evidence id="spuk.employees.fy2024" />.

Two cautions before you do anything with that. It's one subsidiary, and its turnover includes services it invoices to the rest of the group, so it isn't UK merchant revenue <Evidence id="spuk.structure.fy2024" />. And its margin means nothing: administrative expenses exceed turnover and the entity is profitable only because of £58 million of interest income <Evidence id="spuk.operating-profit.fy2024" />. That's a transfer pricing arrangement, not a business.

## Headcount

The last figure Stripe published was "almost 7,000" in February 2022, in the email announcing a 14% cut <Evidence id="headcount.feb-2022" />. Nothing since. I've left it unestablished.

If you take one thing from this page: the numbers that matter most, revenue and cash flow, are real but second hand. The two that are audited describe a UK plumbing entity. Stripe's own letters are candid and specific, and I use them throughout, but they're the company describing itself.
